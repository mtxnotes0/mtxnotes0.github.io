How the note moves.

Nothing is stored on a server. The chain is the ledger.

01

Connect

MetaMask injects the provider. The site reads the account and asks for the encryption public key only at payment.

02

Pay the contract

buy() sends the ETH and stores that key. A second buy reverts until paidAt plus newNoteDuration().

03

Deliver, then decrypt

Only the author encrypts the page and calls deliver(). The buyer calls eth_decrypt.
